How to play:

COMPLETELY OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED STEPS " * " :-
*Sit in a quiet isolated place/room
* use headphones/earphones with a low-mid volume
*a dim/ dark environment
*Laptop/PC or any device with a medium/big screen is STRONGLY RECOMMENDED for the experience

-there are a very few buttons and almost no to little complexity in use. User just needs to press the buttons as the story continues

-The goal is to read the story carefully, and remember all details regardless of the jumpscares

-later those details will be needed to fill after the story.
